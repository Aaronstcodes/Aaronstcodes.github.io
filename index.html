About

Sixteen. Self-taught.
Unreasonably curious.

I'm a Class 10 ICSE graduate from Bangalore, heading into Class 11 at St. Joseph's Boys' High School. I've been building on the web since I was 10, and I haven't really stopped.

My interests run from cybersecurity and website building to commercial aviation. Long-term, I'm targeting a job as a pilot one day and eventually flying the A350-1000. Short-term: writing code, learning Python, and playing too much guitar.

I run KDE Neon, know HTML/CSS/JS, and am building out my Python. I love gaming now and then, reading, and I have a growing obsession with aviation systems and how they work.
